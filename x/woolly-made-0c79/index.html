The 3 fixes we'd make first

1The homepage is slow to show its main image

What we saw

On a phone, your homepage took about 12 seconds to show its main image in our test. Google rates anything over 4 seconds as poor. It took about 12 seconds in both test runs.

What to do

Size the main image for phones and load it first, then go through the 7 outside services that load on the homepage, remove the ones you no longer use and load the rest after the page appears.

What it means for sales

Most shoppers find you on a phone. While the first screen is still loading, some of them tap back to the search results and pick another store.

2Quick questions have nowhere to go

Nonechat or assistant on the homepage or product page
What we saw

We didn't find a chat or assistant on mobile, so a shopper with a quick question about shipping or a product has to email and wait. We checked the homepage and a product page on a phone-sized screen (390 pixels wide), scrolled to the bottom.

What to do

Add a chat that answers from your own shipping, returns and product pages, day and night, and passes anything else to your team by email. Start with the questions you already get most.

What it means for sales

A question that waits for an email reply is a sale on hold, and many shoppers move on while they wait. An answer in the moment keeps them on the page.

3Product pages don't show customer reviews

Nonereviews or stars on the product page we checked
What we saw

We didn't see customer reviews on your product pages. For a first-time visitor, reviews are the quickest proof. We looked for a review widget, star ratings and review counts on the homepage and a product page after they fully loaded on a phone.

What to do

Turn on a reviews app, ask recent buyers for a review by email a few days after delivery, and show the star rating next to the price.

What it means for sales

First-time visitors don't know your store yet. Reviews from other buyers are the quickest proof that a product is what it looks like, so fewer people leave to look for that proof elsewhere.

What's already working

  • A short menu. 25 links, easy to scan on a phone.
  • Product data for Google. Product pages carry schema, so search results can show price and stock.
  • A title and description for Google. The homepage has both, so search results read well.
  • A page that holds still. Layout shift 0.00, under Google's 0.1 limit: nothing jumps under the thumb.
