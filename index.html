FAQ

Questions we get asked

Is the Mini X-Ray really free?

Yes. No card, no login, and no sales call. It's the most honest way we know to show how we think. If you want help fixing what we find, ask. If not, the X-Ray is yours to keep.

What's the difference between the Mini X-Ray and the Full X-Ray?

The Mini X-Ray is a quick, free look from the outside: a score from 0 to 100, the 3 biggest issues and a 3-minute video, in 24 hours. The Full X-Ray ($490, 5 business days) is a complete audit of design, menu and categories, technical SEO, mobile speed, checkout and support. It comes with a prioritized fix list, a redesigned homepage screen and a 45-minute call. The $490 is credited in full toward any package you book within 30 days.

Do you need access to my store?

Not for the Mini X-Ray. We only look at what your customers see. For the Full X-Ray and for projects, we'll ask for collaborator or staff access so we can review your settings, apps and checkout. You can remove our access at any time.

Which platforms do you work with?

We build stores on Shopify. Websites without a store can be built in Framer, Webflow or WordPress. If you're on another platform, the Mini X-Ray still works, and we'll tell you honestly whether moving makes sense.

How long does a project take?

Quick fixes take 1 to 2 weeks. A Brand Refresh takes 3 weeks, a Website Redesign 4, a Store Redesign 2, a Full Rebrand 6 to 8, and a Full Store Rebuild 8. The clock starts once we have your content and access.

Are the prices fixed? Is anything extra?

The prices on our site cover the scope described for each service. Third-party costs are separate and paid directly to the provider: paid themes, apps, fonts, stock photos and support platforms like Gorgias or Tidio. If you ask for something outside the scope, we quote it before we start.

Will an AI agent talk to my customers on its own?

It answers from what you approve: your policies, FAQ and order information. When a question falls outside that, or a customer asks for a person, it hands the conversation to your team. Every month you get a report of what customers asked, so you can check its answers and fill gaps.
