01 · The challenge

Good boots,
sold like clearance.

Cobble makes boots, loafers and everyday shoes, and has resoled them at its own bench since 1987. Its 2016 WooCommerce store told a different story: a clip-art logo, a “Fall Sale! Up to 40% off” slideshow, and wide widths “by special order, please call the store”.

02 · Brand refresh

Keep the name and the family.
Lose the clip art.

The new logo sets Cobble in Hoefler Text Black, a heavy old-style serif with the weight of a stacked heel, over a Gill Sans line that says what and since when. It is drawn as outlines, so it never depends on a font being installed.

Around it, a small system from the workshop: leather and kraft colours, a heel-nail pattern and a heel stamp for every resoled pair.

Palette

Welt black, cordovan, saddle tan, eyelet brass, shoebox kraft, glove lining and tissue paper.

Type

Libre Caslon Text and Cabin, both free web fonts, self-hosted in 3 files (51 KB).

Voice

The person at the repair counter: plain, specific, a little dry.

Built around one question:
will they fit?

Navigation

41 menu links became 5: Boots, Shoes, Loafers, Resoling and Our Story. The home page opens on the best seller, 1 tap from the first screen instead of 2.

Size and width

Collections filter by width first and every card lists D, E and EE. On the product page, size buttons and a width selector sit next to a fit finder that compares a pair you already own.

Product page & cart

Under the button: free shipping, free exchanges for 60 days, and resoling for $95. The cart drops the coupon box and the surprise shipping line, confirms the size and width, and repeats the exchange promise.

Migration, SEO & speed

05 · AI support agent

Fit at 11 pm.
A resole by email.

The agent works in the site chat and the help inbox, and answers from Cobble’s own data: fit notes for each last, the size chart, stock, orders, the exchange policy and the resoling price list. When a rule says so, it hands the conversation to a person with a three-line summary.

01Size and width per style, from the fit finder’s rules
02Order status, once the email on the order is confirmed
03Resoling quotes and exchanges, from the shop’s rules
04Hands off orthotic questions and damage claims
